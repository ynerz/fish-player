## 一、固定段落（每条鱼都一样）

```
LIGHT = strong rim light along the back and tail, no ambient fill light, deep unlit shadow side, high contrast between the lit edge and the shadow, high-end product render look.
GEOM  = Finely faceted low-poly surface, a dense triangular polygon mesh covering the body, crisp visible polygon edges, flat shading per facet with subtle tone variation between neighbouring facets, no texture, no gradients.
BG    = Plain dark neutral grey background, completely empty, no scenery, no text, no watermark.
```
⚠️ `GEOM` 在**闪光档**换成更粗的大块面措辞（防小像素块）；`LIGHT` 只对**非鱼体型**换掉开头的部件锚点（水母 / 八腕 / 鲸，见下表），后半 4 句逐字不变。

## 三、五档颜色句：**每条鱼实际会抽到的那一套**

颜色句每档有 **5 套候选**（`MORPH_CANDIDATES`），`MORPH_POOL` 给权重，
出图时由 `morph_pick(鱼 id, 档)` **按 md5 稳定加权抽一套** —— 同一条鱼重跑永远是同一套（可复现），**不同鱼之间才会不一样**。
